## 4. Couldn't Do / Uncertain

- **Haptic feedback API**: Standard web haptics (`navigator.vibrate`) vary across devices and iOS Safari does not support `vibrate()`. Long-press feedback for `'easy'` grade relies on visual scale-down (`scale(0.97)`) and background color deepening while held, which works reliably across all browsers.
