# SPEAK v8: levels, Know it, topic runs and the new Ask box
State: sealed 2026-11-10

## What "done" looks like
> Scope: the v8 handoff (`.claude/handoffs/2026-11-10-speak-v8-levels-topics.md`) and the locked picks in memory `speak-v8-difficulty-topics.md`. The trim job (speak-trim-to-feed-favorites) is finished; its work is on disk, uncommitted, on `v4-usable`.

The Feed skips the easiest cards at the start, every one of the 1,500 cards carries a level (1-3) and a topic label (Money · Emotions · Work · People · Food · Health · Home · Travel · Senses & weather · General), cards come in short runs of one topic with 👍 topics more often, and one tap on "Know it" drops the easier cards at once; about 10 Know-its in a module bring 30 harder, twice-checked AI cards to the front. A 4th tab, Ask, is one text box: he types feedback or a rant, the AI answers in a line or two, and what it learned (wishes, new topics, level moves) shows under Me with Remove and steers the Feed and the next batch at once.
Proof: each pass's own small check, then one final run of every unit test (2 workers), both type checks, the rulebook check, the two browser specs, the build with its offline file, and fresh 390x844 pictures of Feed, Favorites, Ask and Me. No push, no deploy.
