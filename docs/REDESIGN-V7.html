1 · What is wrong today

Six reasons the app feels confusing

Too many card styles in one feed

The feed draws from about 1,300 cards of 20 different styles: words, swaps, idioms, phrases, feelings, action verbs, pronunciation, breathing, "say it" lines, describe, explain, teach-back, situations and the new lessons. You can't tell what a card is for.

Proof: 23 content files in the app; only 8 of them were written for the current design.

Every card is forced into one shape

A simple word still gets a rule slide, an analogy slide and a "your turn" slide. Vocabulary should be word, meaning, example. It isn't.

Proof: the slide maker builds the same 4–5 slides for every lesson card.

It teaches theory, not lines you can use

Small talk opens with "They like you more than you think" and a research credit. You wanted replies you can say tomorrow at the coffee machine.

Proof: the first small-talk lesson is a mindset card citing Boothby et al. (2018).

Some English is too heavy

"Wary" is defined as "keen caution about possible dangers or hidden traps", with "calculated, tactical vigilance before committing resources". Nobody talks like that.

Proof: the "feelings" deck, 80 cards in this style.

Repeats are built in

Only 20 new cards are allowed per day. After that, the endless feed quietly refills with cards you have already seen. The same fix is also taught in several places: "revert" in 2 files, "do the needful" in 2, "doubt" in 9.

Proof: the daily limit is set to 20 new cards in the feed rules.

The swipe doesn't follow your finger

The card moves only about half as far as your finger, lags a little, then vanishes, and the next card just appears. Slides fade in with a tiny nudge instead of sliding across. On the last slide, a sideways swipe suddenly jumps to the next card.

Proof: the card moves at 45% of your finger, with a 0.22-second delay.

2 · The new shape

Five modules, one card shape each

A row of chips sits at the top of the feed. Mix rotates through all five. Tap a chip to see only that module. Each module has its own colour and one fixed card shape, so after a few swipes you always know what you're looking at. Cards have 1 to 3 slides, never more.

Mix Words Small talk Say it better Office phrases Stories

On every card: swipe up for the next card and sideways for more slides. The side buttons are 👍 Like, 👎 Dislike, 💬 Comment (type or speak), ♡ Save and 🔊 Hear it. The "Again / Got it" buttons are removed: swiping up means you got it, and saving brings a card back.

5 · Swipe and animation

The whole card moves with your finger

Try this phone: scroll up to change cards and swipe sideways to change slides. It's built the way the real app would be.

Today

  • The card moves about half as far as your finger
  • It trails your finger slightly
  • The next card pops in from nowhere
  • Slides fade in place
  • A sideways swipe on the last slide jumps to a new card

New

  • The card sticks to your finger
  • The next card is already waiting underneath and slides up with it
  • Let go past about a quarter of the screen, or flick, and it snaps into place
  • Slides move across like an Instagram carousel
  • Sideways only changes slides; up only changes cards
  • Swipe down to go back

How: it uses the phone's own built-in scrolling, the same thing Instagram and every photo gallery use. Because the phone does the work, it stays smooth even on a slow connection.

6 · The life of a card

A card shows once. What you tell it decides what comes next.

You're right that a fixed launch set alone would run dry and start repeating. So the launch library is only the starting stock. From then on, new batches are written from your 👍, 👎 and comments.

1WaitingIn the library, never shown to you
→
2Shown onceThe app records the card and its word or phrase, forever
→
Swiped pastDone. It never comes back.
👍 LikedDone. The next batch makes more like it.
♡ SavedComes back in the Sunday review: after 3 days, then 1 week, then 3 weeks. Get it right 3 times and it retires.
👎 DislikedRemoved. Your reason is passed on to the next batch.

How new batches are made

  1. The trigger: when a module has fewer than 60 unseen cards left, the app orders a fresh batch of 30. You never run dry, and nothing is replayed.
  2. What the writer is told: your recent 👍 and 👎 cards, your comments in your own words ("too basic", "more cricket examples"), your level per module, and the full list of every word and phrase you've already seen, so it can't repeat one.
  3. The checker: a second AI checks every card (natural English, correct meaning, ✗-use line present, not a repeat) before it reaches you. Anything doubtful is thrown away.
  4. You see the effect: under Me, a short "What the app learned from you" list, such as "More advanced words · fewer office examples · you like cricket". You can delete any line.
