⚡ Interactive Architecture & Wireframes

SPEAK 2.0 — Casual, Swipeable & Searchable

Redesigned from a rigid 20-minute clinical exam into a delightfully casual daily pocket companion. High-value search, card browsing anywhere, and modular voluntary speaking practice.

⚠️ Previous Experience (Why it broke)

  • Forced 20-minute, 6-block linear exam on app open
  • Mandatory microphone; unusable on commute, sofa, or bed
  • 370+ awesome idioms, swaps & Hindi cards buried in code
  • Zero search bar and zero category browsing
  • Clinical decibel meters and speech pathology jargon

✨ New Experience (Simple, Casual & Useful)

  • Opens immediately to a casual bite-sized card (30s habit)
  • Instant universal search across all 370+ idioms, swaps & Hindi
  • Swipeable decks you can browse silently anywhere, anytime
  • Modular Voice Gym with visual scenario prompts (not blank mic screens)
  • Save to Toolkit for quick review before meetings
