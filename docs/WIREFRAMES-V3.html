Wireframes · V3 · 2026-08-19

The casual rebuild

Same theme, same tokens, same 368 authored cards. Different shape. The feed becomes the product, the microphone becomes optional, and nothing on screen claims anything the app did not measure. Frames are drawn at 1:1, 375×812. Read alongside docs/PRODUCT-V3.md.

What these frames are fixing

The app dead-ends after three cards. Daily runs a 3-item "Core" queue and never hands off to endless — tap through it and you sit on "Loading today's deck…" forever. The counter reads "Core 4/3" on the way there.

The feedback is fabricated. Every recording returns the same two hardcoded sentences over a CSS waveform, and "Save & Done" saves nothing. The 30-second Rapid Rep never opens the microphone at all.

The first card is a breath drill captioned "Tap to reveal workplace meaning & context", with the raw enum straw printed as its subtitle.

What is kept

The token palette and every visual style below is the current one. The SM-2 scheduler, the queue's anti-repetition rules, Dexie + outbox + sync, audioMeter.ts and its calibration math, useMissionAudio, TTS, and all 368 authored cards carry over untouched.

Two decisions before Antigravity starts

1 · The name. Recommendation: Articulate, with Speak surviving as one tab. Nothing in the build depends on it.

2 · Describe-this images. Recommendation: a curated static pack of ~150 images shipped with the app — free, instant, offline, and reliably worth describing — rather than runtime generation, which costs per view and adds 3–8 s of latency to a casual app.

Frames are 1:1 at 375×812. Palette, type and shadows are the current tokens — no theme change is proposed.
