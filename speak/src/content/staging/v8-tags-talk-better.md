## How levels were set
- Small talk: a plain "How was it?" reply or a friendly opener is level 1. Asking a follow-up question, or leaving a chat politely, is level 2. Formal or client moments, awkward recoveries (wrong name, spilled coffee, touchy topic, weight comment) and refusing something firmly are level 3.
- Say it better: short one-line fixes (reply by Friday, "I have a question") are level 1. A reason plus a trade-off, a polite disagreement, saying no with an offer, or explaining tech to a non-tech listener is level 2 or 3. Level 3 is for the ones where a wrong word costs something (client, boss, money, family).
