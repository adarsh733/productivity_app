## How levels were set
- Phrases: existing levels kept. Read for "plainly wrong"; none found (idioms like "piece of cake" at L1 and "Per my last email" at L3 both sit right).
- Stories: every story is 19-32 words, so length does not separate them. Level follows what the learner must do: L1 = one plain everyday event, L2 = a twist, a number or a punchline to land, L3 = abstract: a business pitch built on a gap or a feeling, a planted-and-returned line, a negotiation or a promise with a number.
- All 16 business-idea cards are L2 or L3 (pitching is harder than telling your day).
